SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) Global Feature Importance

              
Rigorous game-theoretic attribution quantifying each biomarker's average marginal contribution

            

          

          

            The model relies most heavily on Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c), Fasting Blood Glucose, Systolic BP, Mean Arterial Pressure, and the Cholesterol-to-HDL ratio. This aligns closely with clinical guidelines for cardiometabolic disease etiology.
          

          

            

              
Key Positive Risk Drivers

              
    
                
  • HbA1c & Fasting Glucose: Primary indicators of chronic insulin resistance.
    • 
                
  • Systolic BP & Pulse Pressure: Reflects hemodynamic shear stress and arterial stiffness.
    • 
                
  • Triglyceride-Glucose (TyG) Index: Independent marker of peripheral metabolic dysfunction.
    • 
                
  • Active Smoking: Multiplies atherogenic plaque progression risk.
    • 
              

            


            

              
Protective Biomarkers

              
    
                
  • HDL Cholesterol (> 50 mg/dL): Promotes reverse cholesterol transport.
    • 
                
  • High Physical Activity: Ameliorates resting heart rate and arterial compliance.
    • 
                
  • Normal Hemoglobin: Rules out secondary anemia-related cardiac strain.
    • 
                
  • Healthy BMI (< 25): Minimizes visceral adiposity and systemic inflammation.
    • 
              

            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
          

            

              

                
PCA: Linear Variance Manifold

                
Cumulative Explained Variance: 68.4%

              

            

            

              
            

            

              PCA projects the 27-dimensional feature space along orthogonal eigenvectors capturing maximal variance.
            

          


          
          

            

              

                
UMAP: Non-Linear Topological Manifold

                
Cluster preservation via fuzzy simplicial sets

              

            

            

              
            

            

              UMAP isolates distinct clinical phenotypes, cleanly separating healthy patients from severe metabolic syndrome.
            

          

        


        

          
PCA vs UMAP Methodological Comparison

          

            PCA (Principal Component Analysis) is optimal for global variance decomposition and linear subspace projection without distorting absolute metric distances. 
            In contrast, UMAP (Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection) excels at identifying subtle local non-linear clinical sub-phenotypes and clustering patterns that linear transformations obscure.

About the Multimodal Medical AI Platform

          

            This system is an advanced research and educational prototype designed to demonstrate multimodal artificial intelligence in healthcare. 
            It unifies unstructured clinical documents (PDF reports, scanned laboratory images) with structured demographic and hemodynamic data, 
            executing rigorous zero-leakage preprocessing, 7-model machine learning benchmarking, SHAP game-theoretic explainability, and 2D/3D clinical manifold visualization.
          


          

            
            

              MANDATORY REGULATORY & ETHICAL DISCLAIMER:

              This system is strictly an academic and scientific prototype. It is NOT certified as a medical device (CE-mark / FDA 510(k)). 
              It does not diagnose diseases, recommend prescriptions, or replace the diagnostic judgment of a qualified licensed healthcare practitioner.
            

          


          

            

              
Multimodal Architecture Highlights

              
    
                
  • Early Multimodal Fusion: Merges PDF text, OCR tables, and vitals into a single standardized vector.
    • 
                
  • Negation-Aware NLP: Differentiates affirmed symptoms from explicitly denied conditions.
    • 
                
  • Model Benchmarking: Evaluates XGBoost, Random Forest, SVM, KNN, Gradient Boosting, Logistic Regression, and MLP.
    • 
                
  • Explainable AI: Integrates local instance waterfalls and global SHAP summary values.
    •
